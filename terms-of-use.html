Terms of Use

By accessing or using the QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System for Borongan City, users agree to use the website and system responsibly and only for their intended purposes.

Proper Use

Users should use the system only for legitimate transportation-related transactions, account management, information access, and other functions authorized by the system.

Account Responsibility

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and for all activities performed using their account.

Users should immediately report any suspected unauthorized access or incorrect account information.

Accurate Information

Drivers and authorized users are expected to provide accurate and updated information when registering or updating records.

False, misleading, or unauthorized information should not be entered into the system.

Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

  • Access another person's account without authorization
  • Attempt to bypass system security
  • Modify or manipulate transaction records without authorization
  • Enter false information
  • Misuse another user's personal information
  • Attempt to interfere with the operation of the system
  • Use the system for unauthorized or illegal purposes

Transactions and Records

All recorded transactions should reflect legitimate activities performed through the authorized system process.

Account loading and payment-related activities must follow the procedures established by the authorized office and cashier.

System Availability

The system is intended to support transportation-related record management and transactions. Temporary unavailability may occur because of maintenance, technical problems, or other circumstances.

Changes to the System

System features, procedures, and terms may be updated when necessary to improve the system or to comply with applicable requirements and instructions from the responsible office.

Contact

For questions, concerns, or assistance regarding the system, users may contact the appropriate office through the official contact information provided on the Contact Us section.

Note: These terms are for the proposed academic system and should be reviewed and approved by the appropriate office before actual government implementation.

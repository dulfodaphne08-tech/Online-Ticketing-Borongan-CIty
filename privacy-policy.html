Privacy Policy

The QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System for Borongan City respects the privacy of its users and is committed to protecting the personal information collected through the system.

Information We Collect

The system may collect information necessary for driver registration, vehicle identification, account management, and transaction recording, including:

  • Full name
  • Address
  • Contact information
  • Birthdate, when required
  • Gender, when required
  • Driver or license information, when required
  • Vehicle information
  • Body number
  • Plate number
  • Username and account information
  • Transaction and account balance records

Only information necessary for the operation of the system should be collected.

How We Use Information

The information collected may be used to:

  • Register and identify drivers and vehicles
  • Manage driver accounts
  • Generate driver identification and QR codes
  • Record transportation transactions
  • Manage account balances and authorized account loading
  • Generate reports and transaction history
  • Monitor and manage system records

Protection of Information

Reasonable security measures should be implemented to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse. Access to information should be limited to authorized users according to their assigned system roles.

Data Retention

Transaction and registration records may be retained according to the requirements and record-keeping practices applicable to the office and the system.

User Responsibility

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential and for immediately reporting suspected unauthorized access or incorrect account information.

Privacy Concerns

For questions or concerns regarding personal information and privacy, users may contact the appropriate office through the official contact information provided on the Contact Us section.

Note: This privacy policy is for the proposed academic system and should be reviewed and approved by the appropriate office before actual government implementation.

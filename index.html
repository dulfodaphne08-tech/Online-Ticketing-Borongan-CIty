ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Designed for Better Transport Management

In many transportation terminals, drivers pay a fee when it is their turn in the queue. The current process is done manually on paper — which can cause delays, errors, misplaced records, and difficulty retrieving past transactions. The proposed system provides an organized, digital alternative for managing terminal ticketing and fee collection in Borongan City.

Driver Identification

Maintain organized digital records for registered drivers and their public utility vehicles.

QR Verification

Verify registered driver and vehicle information using a unique QR code at the terminal.

Fee Management

Automatically compute, record, and issue a receipt for transportation fee transactions.

Mission & Vision

Mission

The bctt in collaboration with different government offices and public transport cooperatives/associations shall endeavor to develop and promote the transportation industry as source of livelihood among drivers and operators; ensure the safety, comfort, convenience, and well being of the riding public through the provision of responsive, accountable and God centered regulatory services.

Vision

Consistent with the Vision and Mission of the City Government, the BCTT envisions for a progressive and self-sustaining inter-modal public land transport interchange with God-fearing, highly dedicated and flexible personnel ready to embrace an innovative transportation technology and working towards an inclusive transport business opportunities which shall spur an environment-friendly and sustainable socio-economic growth.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the QR-Based Transport Ticketing System?

It is a web-based platform that digitizes the registration, identification, and fee collection of public utility vehicle drivers at the Borongan City Transport Terminal.

Who can use the system?

Registered drivers of tricycles, jeepneys, multicabs, and buses; and authorized terminal officers and administrators.

What is the QR code used for?

The QR code is used to instantly identify the registered driver and vehicle when it is their turn in the queue at the terminal.

How can a driver register?

Drivers register through the system portal by providing the required personal and vehicle information, after which a QR code is generated.

How is the transportation fee computed?

When the QR code is scanned, the system identifies the vehicle type and automatically computes the corresponding fee based on the configured fee schedule.

Who can perform QR verification?

Only authorized terminal officers designated by the Borongan City Transport Terminal may perform QR verification.

Are transaction records saved?

Yes. Every completed transaction is stored in the system's database and can be viewed, searched, and monitored by authorized users.

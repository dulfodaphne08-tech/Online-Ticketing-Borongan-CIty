Core Modules of the Ticketing System

The system consists of interconnected modules that support driver registration, QR identification, terminal verification, authorized fee processing, digital transaction records, and administrative reporting.

02

How the Modules Connect

Registration QR Identification QR Scanning Fee Processing Transaction Records Reports

The modules are not isolated features. Each one passes information to the next, creating one consistent record for each registered driver and terminal transaction. Registration creates the driver account and generates the Driver ID and QR code. QR Identification links the QR code to the driver's registered record. QR Scanning retrieves the correct driver record during terminal verification. Fee Processing processes the applicable terminal fee against the driver's prepaid balance. Transaction Records store the completed activity in the centralized database, and Reports & Monitoring allow authorized administrators to review and analyze recorded activity.

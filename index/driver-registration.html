Driver & Vehicle Registration

The QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System provides a centralized registration process for public utility vehicle drivers operating at the Borongan City Transport Terminal (BCTT).

The registration module supports the digital onboarding of public utility vehicle drivers at the Borongan City Transport Terminal — covering submission of driver and vehicle information, verification, approval, and the generation of a unique Driver ID and QR code for authorized terminal identification and transactions.

01

Registration Overview

The registration process allows authorized personnel to record driver and vehicle information, verify submitted details, create a system account, and generate a Driver ID and QR code for authorized terminal identification and transaction processing.

02

What the System Records

The registration process may record the following information:

  • Driver's full name
  • Address
  • Contact information
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • Vehicle type
  • Plate number
  • Driver's license information
  • Username and account credentials
  • Registration status

The information collected should be limited to information necessary for the operation and authorized use of the system.

03

Who Can Register

The registration process is intended for public utility vehicle drivers who need to be registered in the Borongan City Transport Terminal system.

Applicants are subject to the applicable verification and approval procedures of the responsible terminal or office.

Registration in the system does not by itself establish legal authority to operate a public utility vehicle.

04

Registration Options

Online Registration

Drivers who have access to a cellphone or computer may complete the registration process through the available online registration page. The applicant provides the required information and submits the registration for review.

Assisted Registration

Drivers who do not have access to a cellphone, computer, or internet connection may request assistance from authorized personnel at the BCTT Operations Office.

Authorized personnel may assist the applicant in entering the required information into the system. The applicant should provide the required information and documents for verification.

Important

Assisted registration does not bypass the normal verification and approval process.

05

Information and Documents to Prepare

Applicants should prepare the information or documents required by the responsible terminal office.

  • Valid government-issued identification
  • Driver's license
  • Vehicle registration information
  • Plate number
  • Address
  • Contact information
  • Other information required by the responsible office

Actual requirements may vary depending on the official registration procedures.

06

Registration Process

1
Submit Registration

The driver or authorized personnel enters the required driver and vehicle information into the registration form.

2
Information Verification

Authorized personnel review the submitted information and check the required details.

3
Account Approval

After the information has been reviewed and approved according to the applicable procedure, the driver's account may be activated.

4
Driver ID & QR Code

The system generates a unique Driver ID and QR code associated with the driver's registered system record.

5
Account Access

Once the account is active, the driver may log in and view available information.

07

Driver ID and QR Code

After successful registration and approval, the system associates a unique QR code with the driver's registered record.

The QR code is used for authorized driver identification and verification at the terminal. Authorized terminal staff can scan the QR code using the designated scanner to retrieve the driver's registered information.

The QR code should not be treated as a replacement for a driver's license or other government-issued authorization.

08

What Happens After Registration?

Driver Account
The driver's account becomes available for authorized system access.

Driver ID
A unique Driver ID is associated with the driver's record.

QR Code
A unique QR code is generated for terminal identification and verification.

Prepaid Balance
If the prepaid transaction function is applicable, the driver may load an authorized prepaid amount through the designated cashier process.

Terminal Transactions
The QR code may be used during authorized terminal transactions.

Transaction History
Completed transactions may appear in the driver's account and centralized terminal records.

09

Registration Status

A driver's registration may have different statuses depending on the system configuration and approval process.

Pending

The registration has been submitted and is waiting for authorized personnel to review the information.

Approved / Active

The driver's information has been verified and the account has been activated.

Rejected

The submitted information does not meet the applicable requirements or requires further action.

Inactive

The account is not currently active for system access or authorized transactions.

Drivers should contact authorized terminal personnel if they have questions about their registration status.

10

Correction of Driver Information

If incorrect information is entered during registration, the driver should report the error to authorized personnel.

Authorized administrators may review and correct driver information according to the system's access permissions.

Examples of information that may require correction include:

  • Incorrect name
  • Incorrect address
  • Incorrect contact information
  • Incorrect vehicle type
  • Incorrect plate number
  • Incorrect license information

Changes to important records should be properly authorized and recorded.

11

Drivers Without a Cellphone

Drivers who do not have a cellphone should not be excluded from the registration process.

They may visit the BCTT Operations Office during the designated registration schedule and request assisted registration.

Authorized personnel can enter the required information into the system on the applicant's behalf, subject to the applicable verification and approval procedures.

Once the registration is approved, the driver may receive the necessary Driver ID and QR code according to the terminal's established procedure.

12

Important Security Reminder

Drivers should protect their account information.

Do not:

  • Share your password with other people.
  • Allow unauthorized persons to use your account.
  • Give your Driver ID to another driver.
  • Use another driver's QR code.
  • Create duplicate accounts without authorization.

If a QR code, Driver ID, or account appears to have been misused, report the issue to authorized terminal personnel.

13

Important System Boundary

Driver registration in the system is intended to support digital recordkeeping, driver identification, QR verification, prepaid balance management, and authorized terminal transactions.

Registration does not automatically mean that the driver is legally authorized by the government to operate a public utility vehicle.

Driver authorization, registration approval, vehicle requirements, and other applicable requirements remain subject to the procedures and approval of the responsible terminal or government office.

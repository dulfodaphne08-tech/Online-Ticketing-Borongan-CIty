Data Privacy Policy

Guidelines concerning the collection, use, protection, access, retention, and management of information handled by the QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System.

Policy Document Drivers · Cashiers · Staff · Administrators

This Data Privacy Policy explains how information handled by the Borongan City Transport Terminal QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System is intended to be collected, used, protected, and managed — covering driver registration, account management, prepaid balance loading, QR-based verification, terminal transactions, and authorized reporting.

—

Policy Overview

This Data Privacy Policy explains how information handled by the Borongan City Transport Terminal QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System is intended to be collected, used, protected, and managed.

The policy applies to information handled through driver registration, account management, prepaid balance loading, QR-based driver verification, terminal transactions, and authorized reporting.

01

Information the System May Handle

Depending on the system function and information required by the responsible office, the system may handle:

Driver Information

  • Full name
  • Address
  • Contact information
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • Driver ID

Vehicle Information

  • Vehicle type
  • Plate number
  • Vehicle-related registration information

License Information

  • Driver's license information
  • License-related records required for driver verification

Account Information

  • Username
  • Password or securely stored authentication information
  • Account status
  • Assigned system role

Transaction Information

  • Transaction ID
  • Transaction date and time
  • Applicable terminal fee
  • Amount deducted
  • Remaining prepaid balance
  • Payment or reference information, when applicable
  • Staff or cashier account associated with the transaction
  • Transaction status

Only information necessary for the applicable system function should be collected.

02

Collection of Information

Information may be collected through authorized system activities, including:

Driver Registration

A driver provides the required information when creating or completing a driver account.

Account Management

Authorized personnel may review or update driver information when corrections are required.

Prepaid Balance Loading

The designated cashier records the driver's prepaid load and applicable payment or reference information.

QR Verification

The driver's QR code is scanned by authorized terminal staff to retrieve the information required for terminal verification.

Terminal Transactions

The system records information necessary to process and document authorized terminal transactions.

Reports and Monitoring

Authorized administrators may access recorded information for operational monitoring and authorized reporting.

03

Purpose of Collection

Information handled by the system is intended to support legitimate system and terminal-management functions, including:

  • Identifying registered drivers
  • Managing driver and vehicle records
  • Generating Driver IDs and QR codes
  • Verifying registered drivers
  • Managing prepaid balances
  • Recording authorized terminal transactions
  • Recording payment or reference information
  • Maintaining transaction history
  • Preparing authorized reports
  • Supporting administrative monitoring
  • Maintaining system accountability

Information should not be collected or used for unrelated purposes without appropriate authorization.

04

QR Code Information

The QR code assigned to a registered driver is intended primarily for system identification and terminal verification.

When scanned by authorized personnel, the system may retrieve information necessary to verify the driver's:

  • Driver ID
  • Name
  • Registered vehicle
  • Plate number
  • Account status
  • Other authorized verification information

The QR code should not provide unrestricted access to the driver's complete account information. Access to detailed information remains controlled by the user's assigned system role.

05

Role-Based Access

Access to information is restricted according to the user's assigned system role.

Driver

The driver may access information associated with their own account: driver profile, Driver ID, QR code, vehicle information, prepaid balance, and transaction history.

Cashier

The cashier may access information necessary to identify the driver, process authorized prepaid loads, record payment/reference information, and review cashier-related loading transactions.

Staff

Authorized staff may access information necessary to scan and verify driver QR codes, confirm driver information, process authorized terminal transactions, and record transaction activity.

Administrator

Authorized administrators may access broader system information required for user management, driver record management, transaction monitoring, fee configuration, reports, audit records, and administrative review.

Users should only access information necessary for their assigned responsibilities.

06

Password and Account Protection

User accounts should be protected using appropriate authentication and security controls.

Users should:

  • Keep passwords confidential
  • Avoid sharing account credentials
  • Use only their assigned account
  • Log out after using shared or terminal workstations
  • Report suspected unauthorized account access

System administrators should apply appropriate access controls to protect account credentials. Passwords should not be displayed or stored in an insecure manner.

07

Data Accuracy and Correction

The system should maintain accurate and updated information.

If incorrect information is discovered, authorized personnel may review and correct the applicable record according to the system's administrative procedures.

For example, an administrator may correct:

  • Driver name
  • Address
  • Contact information
  • Vehicle type
  • Plate number
  • License information
  • Account status

Authorized changes should be properly recorded when audit logging is enabled.

08

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be applied to protect system information against:

  • Unauthorized access
  • Unauthorized modification
  • Unauthorized disclosure
  • Loss or misuse
  • Improper account access

Security measures may include:

  • Role-based access control
  • Account authentication
  • Password protection
  • Database access controls
  • Audit logging
  • Secure system configuration
  • Restricted administrative access

Security controls should be reviewed and improved as the system develops.

09

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to support legitimate:

  • Terminal operations
  • Driver account management
  • Transaction monitoring
  • Financial/reference record management
  • Reporting
  • Administrative review
  • Academic project requirements

Actual retention periods should be determined by the responsible office and applicable institutional or legal requirements. The academic project should not claim a specific official retention period unless one has been formally established.

10

Authorized Disclosure

Information should only be disclosed to authorized individuals or organizations when there is a legitimate purpose and appropriate authorization.

Examples may include:

  • Authorized terminal personnel
  • Authorized administrators
  • Responsible institutional personnel
  • Authorized government personnel, when applicable
  • Other parties when disclosure is required by applicable rules or law

Personal information should not be unnecessarily disclosed to unauthorized individuals.

11

Transaction and Payment Information

The system may record information associated with prepaid balance loading and terminal transactions.

This may include:

  • Load amount
  • Transaction amount
  • Date and time
  • Payment/reference information
  • Cashier responsible for the load
  • Staff responsible for the transaction
  • Remaining balance
  • Transaction status
Important

The system is intended to manage and record authorized payment/reference information and prepaid terminal transactions. It should not be represented as an official government payment processor or official government receipt issuer unless such functions have been formally authorized.

12

Cookies and Technical Information

If the website uses cookies, local storage, session information, or similar technical mechanisms, these may be used to support functions such as:

  • Account sessions
  • Authentication
  • User preferences
  • System functionality
  • Security

Only technical information necessary for the operation of the website should be collected through such mechanisms. If a specific technical mechanism is not actually implemented in the system, it should not be claimed as an existing feature.

13

Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

The system may use third-party technologies or services for technical functions such as database hosting, authentication, software libraries, or system deployment.

Where third-party services are used, information handled through those services should be subject to the applicable security, privacy, and service requirements.

The project team should avoid unnecessarily sharing personal information with third parties.

14

User Responsibilities

Users of the system are expected to help protect their information and account.

Users should:

  • Provide accurate information
  • Protect their login credentials
  • Avoid sharing passwords
  • Use only their assigned account
  • Report incorrect information
  • Report suspected unauthorized access
  • Follow applicable system procedures

Drivers should also keep their Driver ID and QR code reasonably secure and present them only for authorized terminal verification.

15

Privacy Concerns and Requests

Users who have concerns about their information may contact the responsible terminal office or appropriate project/institutional representative.

Concerns may include:

  • Incorrect personal information
  • Unauthorized access
  • Improper disclosure
  • Suspicious account activity
  • Questions regarding information handling

Requests should provide enough information for the responsible personnel to understand and review the concern.

16

Policy Review and Updates

This policy may be reviewed and updated when:

  • System functionality changes
  • New information is collected
  • User roles are modified
  • Security procedures are improved
  • Institutional requirements change
  • Applicable privacy requirements change
  • The responsible office establishes new procedures

The latest approved version should be made available through the website when appropriate.

—

Important Notice

Academic Project Scope

This Data Privacy Policy describes the intended information-handling practices of the academic project.

It does not claim to represent the formal privacy policy, data protection practices, retention schedule, or official procedures of the Borongan City Transport Terminal, Borongan City Government, or any other government office unless those practices have been formally reviewed and adopted by the responsible authority.

Actual collection, processing, retention, disclosure, and protection of personal information should follow the applicable policies, institutional requirements, and laws established by the responsible authority.

This policy should therefore be treated as the privacy framework for the academic system prototype unless and until formally adopted by the responsible office.

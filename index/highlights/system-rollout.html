QR-Based Ticketing System Rollout for Public Utility Vehicles

Project information about the rollout of the QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System for public utility vehicles operating within the Borongan City Transport Terminal.

August 21, 2026 BCTT Main Terminal, Borongan City
BCTT Main Terminal · System Rollout
01

About the Rollout

The QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System is being prepared to support the digital registration, identification, prepaid balance management, and terminal transaction recording of registered public utility vehicle drivers.

The system is designed to support tricycles, jeepneys, multicabs, and buses according to the vehicle types and fee configurations authorized for use by the responsible terminal office.

02

What the Rollout Covers

The rollout includes the preparation and testing of the following system components:

  • Driver and vehicle registration
  • Driver account verification and approval
  • Driver ID and QR code generation
  • QR-based driver identification
  • Designated QR scanning at the terminal
  • Cashier-based prepaid balance loading
  • Payment and reference record management
  • Automatic fee computation based on vehicle type
  • Prepaid balance deduction during authorized transactions
  • Digital transaction recording
  • Driver transaction history
  • Reports and monitoring
  • Role-based access for Drivers, Cashiers, Staff, and Administrators
03

Rollout Preparation

The rollout involves careful preparation of each module before the system is used operationally at the terminal.

1 System Configuration

The system is configured with the supported vehicle types, user roles, fee settings, and required terminal transaction procedures.

2 Driver Registration

The driver registration process is prepared to collect the required driver and vehicle information and create the driver's system record.

3 QR Code Preparation

After the driver's information has been verified and approved, the system generates a unique Driver ID and QR code linked to the driver's registered record.

4 Cashier Preparation

The cashier module is prepared to record authorized prepaid balance loads and associated payment or reference information.

5 Terminal Scanning Preparation

The designated QR scanner is prepared for use by authorized terminal staff to identify registered drivers and begin authorized terminal transactions.

6 Transaction Testing

The system is tested to verify driver identification, balance checking, fee computation, balance deduction, and transaction recording.

7 Reports and Monitoring

Recorded transactions are made available to authorized administrators for monitoring, searching, filtering, and report generation.

04

Supported User Roles

The system uses role-based access to separate the functions of each user.

Driver

Views personal registration information, Driver ID, QR code, prepaid balance, and transaction history.

Cashier

Records authorized prepaid balance loads and payment/reference information.

Staff

Uses the designated scanner to verify drivers and process authorized terminal transactions.

Administrator

Manages system users, driver records, fee configurations, transactions, reports, and other authorized administrative functions.

05

Expected Rollout Process

The intended operational flow is:

Driver Registration Verification & Approval Driver ID & QR Generation Prepaid Loading QR Scanning Driver Verification Balance Checking Fee Deduction Transaction Recording Reports & Monitoring

This workflow allows the different system roles to work together while maintaining centralized digital records.

06

Expected Benefits

The rollout is intended to help:

  • Reduce manual recording of terminal transactions
  • Provide faster driver identification through QR scanning
  • Reduce manual fee calculation
  • Maintain centralized driver and transaction records
  • Provide a more organized prepaid balance process
  • Make transaction history easier to review
  • Support administrative monitoring and reporting
  • Improve accountability through role-based access and transaction records
07

Rollout Status

The system rollout involves configuration, testing, and preparation of the different modules before operational use.

System functions should only be considered officially operational after the appropriate testing, approval, and implementation procedures of the responsible terminal office have been completed.

Important Project Notice

This page describes the rollout activities and intended workflow of the academic project. Actual implementation, operating procedures, supported vehicle types, fee schedules, schedules, and terminal policies remain subject to the approval of the responsible office.

The system records authorized payment and reference information and manages prepaid balances and terminal transactions. It should not be represented as an official government payment processor or official receipt issuer unless that function has been formally authorized.

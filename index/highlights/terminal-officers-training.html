Featured Highlight August 4, 2026 BCTT Operations Office

Training of Terminal Officers on QR Scanning and Fee Collection

Terminal officers underwent training on using the QR scanning system, verifying driver information, and processing transportation fees in accordance with the approved fee schedule.

August 4, 2026 BCTT Operations Office
BCTT Operations Office · Officer Training
01

Training Overview

The training prepared authorized terminal officers to operate the QR scanning system during their shifts and to process terminal transactions in accordance with the configured procedures.

02

Topics Covered

  • Overview of the QR-based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System
  • Using the designated QR scanner
  • Verifying driver information displayed by the system
  • Checking transaction eligibility and available prepaid balance
  • Processing authorized terminal transactions
  • Recording transaction references when applicable
  • Generating and handling transaction records
03

Verification and Receipt Handling

Training also covered the proper handling of transaction records. Where the system is authorized to produce a receipt or reference record, officers are trained to ensure the correct information is recorded and associated with the correct transaction.

04

Key Procedures

The following 5-step scanning workflow was demonstrated and practiced during the training.

1 Scan the QR Code

Use the designated hardware scanner to read the driver's QR code.

2 Retrieve Driver Record

The system displays the driver's registered information and account status.

3 Verify Eligibility

Confirm the driver's account status, vehicle type, and applicable fee.

4 Process Transaction

Complete the authorized terminal transaction and deduct the fee if applicable.

5 Record and Print

Ensure the transaction is recorded and, if authorized, print a receipt or transaction record.

05

Staff Roles and Responsibilities

The training also introduced the responsibilities of authorized terminal personnel during QR-based terminal operations.

QR Verification

Staff are responsible for using the designated hardware scanner to retrieve and verify the driver's registered information.

Transaction Processing

Staff process authorized terminal transactions after confirming the driver's account status and applicable transaction requirements.

Transaction Recording

Staff ensure that completed transactions are properly recorded in the system and associated with the correct driver.

Record Review

Staff can review relevant transaction information to help identify incomplete, unsuccessful, or recorded transactions that require attention.

06

Hardware and Equipment

The training included familiarization with the equipment used during terminal operations.

Designated QR Scanner

The QR scanner is used by authorized staff to read the driver's QR code and retrieve the corresponding system record.

Terminal Workstation

The workstation provides access to the staff dashboard where driver information and transaction details can be displayed.

Receipt or Transaction Printer

Where applicable and authorized, a designated receipt or transaction printer may be used to produce a transaction record.

Important Note

The system should only be described as issuing an official receipt if this function has been formally authorized by the responsible office. Keep the wording "receipt or transaction record" unless your office has officially authorized your system to issue government official receipts.

07

Transaction Validation

Before completing an authorized terminal transaction, staff are expected to verify the information displayed by the system.

The validation process includes:

  • Confirming the driver's registered identity.
  • Checking the registered vehicle information.
  • Checking the driver's account status.
  • Confirming the applicable vehicle type and configured fee.
  • Checking the available prepaid balance when required.
  • Confirming that the transaction has been successfully recorded.

If the transaction cannot be completed, the staff member should follow the applicable terminal procedure rather than manually changing the system record without authorization.

08

Training Outcome

Following the training, authorized terminal officers are expected to understand the basic workflow for QR-based driver verification and terminal transaction recording.

The training supports consistent use of the designated scanner, accurate driver verification, proper transaction processing, and reliable maintenance of digital terminal records.

These procedures are intended to help ensure that terminal transactions are properly associated with the correct driver and maintained in the centralized transaction database.

Result

Terminal officers are prepared to process authorized terminal transactions using the QR scanning system and to record them in the terminal transaction database for monitoring and reporting.

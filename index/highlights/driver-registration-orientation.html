Featured Highlight August 24, 2026 BCTT Main Terminal

Driver Registration and QR Issuance Orientation

An orientation for public utility vehicle drivers regarding the QR-based driver identification and digital ticketing system. Registered drivers received their unique QR codes, ready for use at the terminal.

August 24, 2026 BCTT Main Terminal, Borongan City
BCTT Main Terminal · Orientation Day
01

Purpose of the Orientation

The orientation was conducted to introduce the QR-based driver identification and digital ticketing system to public utility vehicle drivers operating within the Borongan City Transport Terminal.

02

What Was Discussed

  • Overview of the QR-based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System
  • How driver registration works
  • What information is recorded in the system
  • How the QR code is used for terminal verification
  • How prepaid balance loading is processed through the designated cashier
  • What drivers can see in their account (Driver ID, QR code, transaction history)
03

Driver Registration and QR Issuance

Registered drivers received their unique QR codes, ready for use at the terminal. Each QR code is linked to the driver's authorized system record, which contains the driver's registered information.

Important Note

Driver registration does not automatically mean that the driver is authorized by the government to operate. Approval and verification remain subject to applicable terminal or office procedures.

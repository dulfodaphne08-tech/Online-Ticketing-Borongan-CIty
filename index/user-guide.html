User Guide

A complete guide to using the Borongan City Transport Terminal QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System, organized according to the assigned system roles: Driver, Cashier, Staff, and Administrator.

General Access Driver Cashier Staff Administrator Quick Workflow

This User Guide covers the complete walkthrough of the Borongan City Transport Terminal QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System for each of the four assigned system roles — Driver, Cashier, Staff, and Administrator — including registration, prepaid loading, QR scanning, transaction recording, attendance monitoring, and administrative management.

01

General System Access

Login

All authorized users must sign in using the account credentials assigned to their respective role. The system determines the user's access according to the assigned account role.

Users should not attempt to access dashboards or functions that are not assigned to their account.

Account Roles

The system provides separate access for:

  • Driver
  • Cashier
  • Staff
  • Administrator

Each role has different permissions and responsibilities.

02

For Drivers

Driver Registration

1
Register an Account

Complete the driver registration form using the required personal and vehicle information.

2
Submit Driver and Vehicle Information

Provide full name, address, contact information, birthdate, gender, vehicle type, plate number, driver's license information, and other required registration information.

3
Wait for Verification

After submission, authorized personnel review the driver's information. The account remains subject to the applicable verification and approval process.

4
Account Approval

Once the information has been verified and approved, the driver's account may be activated.

5
Access the Driver Dashboard

Log in using the approved username and password.

6
View Driver Information

The Driver Dashboard may display driver name, Driver ID, vehicle information, plate number, account status, QR code, prepaid balance, and transaction history.

7
View Driver ID and QR Code

The system provides a unique Driver ID and QR code associated with the registered driver record, used for authorized terminal identification and verification.

8
Load Prepaid Balance

The driver loads an authorized prepaid amount through the designated cashier process. The system updates the driver's balance after the load has been successfully recorded.

9
Receive Load Notification

After a successful prepaid load, the driver's dashboard may display an updated balance or system notification.

10
Present the QR Code

When required, present the Driver ID or QR code to authorized terminal staff for scanning.

11
Review Transaction History

The driver can review transaction date, time, applicable fee, amount deducted, remaining balance, transaction status, and reference when applicable.

12
Report Incorrect Information

If any driver information appears incorrect, contact authorized personnel for verification or correction.

03

For Cashiers

Cashier Login

1
Sign In

Sign in using the Cashier account credentials issued by the administrator.

2
Time In

At the beginning of the assigned shift, record the Cashier's Time In. Standard schedule is 8:00 AM. If Time In is recorded after 8:00 AM, the system may display a Late notification.

3
Access the Cashier Dashboard

The dashboard provides access to authorized prepaid loading and payment/reference recording functions.

Prepaid Balance Loading

4
Identify the Driver

Search for or select the registered driver's account. Verify Driver ID, driver name, vehicle information, plate number, and account status.

5
Verify the Driver

Confirm that the driver requesting the prepaid load matches the registered account. Do not load funds into an incorrect account.

6
Enter the Load Amount

Enter the authorized amount that will be added to the driver's prepaid balance.

7
Record Payment Information

Record the applicable payment/reference information, including amount, date, time, reference number, official receipt number when applicable and authorized, and cashier account.

8
Review the Load

Before submitting, verify the correct driver, correct amount, correct reference information, and correct account.

9
Confirm the Load

Confirm the transaction through the designated system function.

10
Update the Driver Balance

The system updates the driver's prepaid balance after the load is successfully recorded.

11
Driver Notification

The driver's dashboard may display the updated balance and a notification indicating that the prepaid load was successfully recorded.

12
Transaction Record

The completed load becomes part of the driver's transaction history and the cashier's transaction records.

13
Time Out

At the end of the assigned shift, record the Cashier's Time Out. Standard time out is 5:00 PM.

Important Cashier Flow

The Cashier verifies the driver first, then records the payment/reference information, and only then confirms the load. This prevents funds from being added to an incorrect account.

04

For Staff

Staff Login

1
Sign In

Sign in using the Staff account credentials issued by the administrator.

2
Time In

Record Time In at the beginning of the assigned shift. Standard Time In is 8:00 AM. If Time In is recorded after 8:00 AM, the system may display a Late notification.

3
Access the Staff Dashboard

The Staff Dashboard provides authorized access to terminal QR scanning and transaction processing.

QR Scanning and Terminal Transaction

4
Prepare the Scanner

Connect the designated hardware QR scanner to the terminal workstation. Ensure the scanner is ready before processing transactions.

5
Present the Driver QR

The driver presents the Driver ID or QR code to the Staff member.

6
Scan the QR Code

Use the designated hardware scanner to scan the driver's QR code.

7
Verify Driver Information

The system retrieves the registered driver information. Verify Driver ID, driver name, vehicle type, plate number, and account status.

8
Check Transaction Eligibility

The system checks the applicable transaction requirements and available prepaid balance when required.

9
Verify the Applicable Fee

The system determines the applicable configured terminal fee based on the registered vehicle type.

10
Process the Transaction

If the transaction meets the configured requirements, proceed with the authorized terminal transaction.

11
Deduct the Applicable Fee

Where prepaid balance is required, the applicable fee is deducted from the driver's available balance.

12
Record the Transaction

The system records the transaction, including driver information, vehicle information, fee, amount deducted, remaining balance, date and time, staff account, and transaction status.

13
Confirm the Transaction

The Staff member verifies that the transaction has been successfully recorded.

14
Provide Transaction Record

Where supported and authorized, provide or print the applicable transaction record.

15
Time Out

At the end of the assigned shift, record Time Out. Standard Time Out is 5:00 PM.

05

For Administrators

Administrator Login

1
Sign In

Sign in using the authorized Administrator account.

2
Time In

Record Time In at the beginning of the assigned shift. Standard Time In is 8:00 AM. If Time In is recorded after 8:00 AM, the system may display a Late notification.

3
Access the Admin Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard provides centralized administrative monitoring and management functions.

06

Admin Dashboard Management

Dashboard Monitoring

4
Monitor System Activity

Review the overall status of registered drivers, registered vehicles, active users, transactions, prepaid loads, system activity, and reports.

Driver Management

5
View Driver Records

Administrators can search and review registered driver information.

6
Edit Incorrect Driver Information

If information entered during registration is incorrect, an authorized administrator can edit the appropriate driver record. Possible corrections include name, address, contact information, birthdate, gender, vehicle type, plate number, license information, and account status.

7
Save the Changes

After the administrator saves the corrected information, the updated record is stored in the centralized database.

8
Driver Dashboard Synchronization

The corrected information automatically reflects in the driver's dashboard because both dashboards use the same centralized driver record. This prevents the Driver Dashboard from displaying outdated information.

Centralized Record Synchronization

When an administrator corrects driver information, the change is stored in the centralized database. Because the Driver Dashboard and Admin Dashboard both reference the same record, the corrected information appears in the driver's account automatically — no separate update step is required.

07

User Management

9
Manage System Users

Administrators may manage authorized Cashier accounts, Staff accounts, and other authorized administrative accounts.

10
Assign User Roles

Administrators can assign the appropriate role according to the user's responsibilities. Each role should only have access to the functions necessary for its assigned duties.

11
Account Status

Administrators may manage account status according to system procedures. Examples include Active, Inactive, and Suspended.

08

Vehicle Management

12
Manage Vehicle Records

Administrators may review registered vehicle information associated with driver accounts, verifying vehicle type, plate number, driver association, and registration status. Changes should only be made when authorized.

09

Fee Configuration

13
Review Fee Configuration

Administrators can review the configured terminal fee schedule.

14
Update Authorized Fees

If the responsible office provides an updated approved fee schedule, the administrator may update the system configuration. The system should use the updated configuration for subsequent transactions.

Vehicle Type Configured Fee
Tricycle₱5
Jeepney₱60
Multicab₱60
Bus₱100

These amounts are only sample/configured values and should be used as official rates only when approved by the responsible office.

10

Transaction Management

15
Review Transactions

Administrators can review recorded terminal transactions, including transaction ID, driver ID, driver name, vehicle type, plate number, applicable fee, amount deducted, remaining balance, staff, date and time, and transaction status.

16
Search and Filter

Administrators can search or filter transactions using driver ID, driver name, plate number, vehicle type, date, transaction status, staff, and transaction ID.

17
Review Transaction Details

Administrators can open individual transaction records to review the information stored in the system.

11

Reports and Monitoring

18
Generate Reports

Administrators can generate authorized reports such as Daily Terminal Report, Monthly Report, Vehicle-Type Report, Driver Transaction Report, Cashier Report, and Staff Transaction Report.

19
Review Historical Records

Administrators can review previous transaction records for authorized monitoring and reporting purposes.

20
Print Reports

Where supported, authorized reports may be printed for administrative or project purposes.

12

Audit Logs

21
Review Audit Activity

Where audit logging is implemented, administrators may review important system activities. Audit information may include user account, user role, action performed, date and time, affected record, previous value, and updated value. This helps maintain accountability and traceability.

13

Attendance Monitoring

The system may provide Time In and Time Out monitoring for Administrators, Cashiers, and Staff.

Standard Schedule

  • Time In: 8:00 AM
  • Time Out: 5:00 PM

Late Notification

If an authorized user records Time In after the configured starting time, the system may display a notification such as:

Late Time In — Your Time In was recorded after 8:00 AM.

Attendance information may be included in authorized administrative reports.

14

Notifications

The system may provide notifications for important account and transaction events.

Driver

  • Account approved
  • Prepaid load successful
  • Balance updated
  • Transaction completed
  • Insufficient balance

Cashier

  • Load successfully recorded
  • Incorrect or incomplete information
  • Transaction confirmation

Staff

  • QR successfully scanned
  • Driver verified
  • Insufficient balance
  • Transaction completed

Administrator

  • New registration
  • Late Time In
  • Account status changes
  • Important system activity
15

Insufficient Balance

If the driver's available prepaid balance is insufficient for an authorized transaction, the system may prevent or flag the transaction according to the configured procedure.

The Staff member should inform the driver that additional authorized prepaid loading may be required. The system should not allow users to bypass the balance validation.

16

Incorrect Information

If incorrect driver information is discovered:

1
Report the Incorrect Information

The driver or authorized personnel reports the incorrect information.

2
Administrator Reviews the Record

An authorized Administrator reviews the affected record.

3
Administrator Edits the Incorrect Information

The Administrator updates the incorrect field through the authorized admin function.

4
Administrator Saves the Changes

The corrected record is saved.

5
Centralized Database Is Updated

The centralized database is updated with the corrected information.

6
Corrected Information Becomes Available to the Driver Dashboard

The Driver Dashboard automatically reflects the corrected information because it reads from the same centralized driver record.

7
Change May Be Recorded in the Audit Log

When audit logging is implemented, the change is recorded with the user account, role, date, time, and affected record.

17

Security Reminders

All users must:

  • Protect their username and password.
  • Never share account credentials.
  • Use only their assigned account.
  • Log out after using a shared workstation.
  • Avoid accessing unauthorized features.
  • Protect Driver IDs and QR codes.
  • Report suspicious activity.
  • Verify information before completing transactions.
18

Important System Boundary

System Scope

The system records authorized payment and reference information and manages prepaid balances and terminal transactions.

It should not be represented as an official government payment processor or official government receipt issuer unless the responsible terminal office has formally authorized those functions.

Driver registration also does not automatically mean that the driver has official government authorization to operate. Actual registration approval, terminal procedures, fees, schedules, and operational requirements remain subject to the responsible office.

Quick Workflow

Driver

Register → Verification → Approval → Driver ID & QR → Load Balance → Present QR → Transaction → View History

Cashier

Login → Time In → Verify Driver → Enter Load → Record Payment/Reference → Confirm → Update Balance → Record Transaction → Time Out

Staff

Login → Time In → Prepare Scanner → Scan QR → Verify Driver → Check Balance → Validate Fee → Process Transaction → Record → Time Out

Administrator

Login → Time In → Monitor → Manage Drivers → Manage Users → Manage Vehicles → Configure Fees → Review Transactions → Generate Reports → Review Audit Logs → Time Out

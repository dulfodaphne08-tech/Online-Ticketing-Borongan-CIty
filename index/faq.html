Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about driver registration, QR identification, prepaid balance, terminal transactions, system access, and administrative functions.

This section provides quick answers to common questions about the Borongan City Transport Terminal QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System. Use the categories below to find the information you need, or visit the Contact Us page for further assistance.

01

Driver Registration

Who can register?

Authorized public utility vehicle drivers who are covered by the applicable Borongan City Transport Terminal registration procedures may register in the system. Registration is subject to information verification and approval by authorized personnel.

Learn more on the Driver & Vehicle Registration page.

Can a driver register without a cellphone?

Yes. Drivers who do not have access to a cellphone or computer may request assisted registration at the BCTT Operations Office, subject to the applicable registration schedule and verification procedures.

What information is required for registration?

The registration process may require the driver's personal information, contact details, vehicle information, plate number, driver's license information, and account credentials. Additional requirements may be requested by the responsible terminal office.

What happens after registration?

The submitted information is reviewed by authorized personnel. Once approved, the driver's account may be activated and the system can generate the driver's Driver ID and associated QR code.

Can I correct my registration information?

Yes. If incorrect information was entered, the driver should report the issue to authorized personnel. An authorized administrator may update the information according to the system's access and verification procedures.

02

Driver ID & QR Code

What is the QR code used for?

The QR code is used to identify and verify a registered driver through the terminal's authorized QR scanning system.

See QR Driver Identification for details.

Where can I use my QR code?

The QR code may be presented during authorized terminal transactions where QR-based driver identification and verification are required.

What should I do if my QR code cannot be scanned?

First, ensure that the QR code is clearly visible and readable. If the QR code still cannot be scanned, request assistance from authorized terminal staff. Staff may verify the driver's information using the available authorized system procedures.

Can another driver use my QR code?

No. A driver's QR code is associated with the driver's registered system record and should only be used by the corresponding registered driver.

03

Prepaid Balance & Fee Collection

Can I load my prepaid balance?

Yes. Prepaid balance loading is processed through the designated cashier procedure. The cashier records the authorized payment and reference information, and the corresponding prepaid balance is updated in the driver's account.

See Fee Collection for details.

Where can I load my prepaid balance?

Prepaid loading is handled through the designated cashier process of the system. Drivers should follow the applicable terminal procedures for loading their account.

What happens when my balance is insufficient?

The system may prevent or flag the transaction when the available prepaid balance is insufficient, subject to the terminal's configured procedures. The driver may need to load additional prepaid balance before completing the applicable transaction.

Can I see my remaining balance?

Yes. When enabled for the driver's account, the current prepaid balance can be viewed through the Driver Dashboard.

Can I see my loading history?

Authorized payment and reference information may be recorded as part of the driver's account and transaction records. The available information depends on the system's configured features and assigned user access.

04

Terminal QR Scanning

Who scans the driver's QR code?

The QR code is scanned by authorized terminal staff using the designated QR scanner.

What happens during QR scanning?

The staff scans the driver's QR code, and the system retrieves the associated registered information for verification. The system may then check the driver's account status, applicable transaction, and available prepaid balance before recording the transaction.

See QR Scanning for the full workflow.

Does QR scanning automatically deduct money?

A balance deduction occurs only when an authorized terminal transaction requiring a fee is successfully processed according to the configured system procedure.

What happens after a successful transaction?

The applicable amount may be deducted from the driver's prepaid balance, and the transaction is recorded in the centralized transaction database.

05

Transaction Records

What information is recorded in a transaction?

Depending on the configured system fields, a transaction may record:

  • Transaction ID
  • Driver ID and driver name
  • Vehicle type and plate number
  • Transaction date and time
  • Applicable fee and amount deducted
  • Remaining balance
  • Payment or cashier reference
  • Staff who processed the transaction
  • Transaction status

See Transaction Records for details.

Can drivers view their transaction history?

Yes. The Driver Dashboard may provide access to the driver's available transaction history.

Who can view transaction records?

Access to transaction information is restricted according to assigned system roles and permissions. Authorized administrators and personnel may have access to transaction records required for their responsibilities.

Can transaction records be changed?

Transaction records should only be modified or managed by authorized personnel according to the system's administrative procedures. Important changes should be properly recorded for accountability.

06

System Roles

Who uses the system?

The system supports different user roles with different responsibilities:

  • Driver — views registered information, Driver ID, QR code, prepaid balance, and transaction history.
  • Cashier — records authorized prepaid loading transactions and payment or reference information.
  • Staff — uses the designated scanner to verify drivers and process authorized terminal transactions.
  • Administrator — manages authorized records, users, system configurations, transactions, reports, and monitoring functions.
Can a driver access the administrative dashboard?

No. Drivers should only have access to functions assigned to the Driver role. Administrative functions are restricted to authorized accounts.

Can staff access cashier or administrator functions?

Access depends on the user's assigned system role and permissions. Users should only access functions necessary for their assigned responsibilities.

07

Reports & Monitoring

What reports can the system generate?

The system can support reports such as:

  • Daily terminal transaction reports
  • Monthly transaction reports
  • Vehicle-type reports
  • Driver transaction reports
  • Cashier loading reports
  • Staff transaction reports

See Reports & Monitoring for details.

Who can generate reports?

Report access is restricted to authorized users, particularly administrative accounts with the appropriate permissions.

Why are reports important?

Reports help authorized personnel monitor terminal activity, review recorded transactions, identify trends, and support administrative decision-making.

08

Account & Security

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the available password recovery procedure or contact the appropriate authorized personnel for account assistance.

Can I share my username and password?

No. Account credentials should be kept confidential and should not be shared with other users.

What happens if my account is inactive?

An inactive account may not be able to access certain system functions or complete authorized transactions. The driver should contact authorized personnel to determine the reason for the inactive status.

Is the driver's information protected?

Access to driver information is restricted according to assigned system roles and authorized system functions. Only information necessary for authorized system operations should be accessible to each user role.

09

Government Receipt & Payment Clarification

Does the system issue an official government receipt?

The system records authorized payment and reference information associated with prepaid loading and terminal transactions.

It should only be described as issuing an official government receipt if the responsible office has formally authorized that function.

Is the prepaid balance the same as a bank or e-wallet account?

No. The prepaid balance in this academic system represents a system-managed balance used for authorized terminal transactions. It should not be presented as a bank account, electronic money account, or commercial e-wallet unless formally authorized and implemented as such.

10

Vehicle Types & Fees

What vehicle types are covered?

The system can be configured for the public utility vehicle types applicable to the terminal, including:

  • Tricycle
  • Jeepney
  • Multicab
  • Bus
How is the terminal fee determined?

The system can determine the applicable fee based on the configured vehicle type and approved fee schedule.

Can the fee schedule be changed?

Authorized administrators may manage the configured fee schedule when this function is enabled. Actual fees must be based on the approved rates provided by the responsible office.

11

Registration and System Assistance

Where can I get assistance?

Drivers who need assistance with registration, QR identification, account access, or other system-related concerns may contact or visit the appropriate BCTT office or authorized personnel.

See the Contact Us page for details.

Can drivers without cellphones use the system?

Yes. Drivers without a cellphone may receive assistance from authorized personnel for registration and other procedures that require system access. The availability of specific services remains subject to the terminal's actual operating procedures.

12

System Boundary

Is this system an official government payment system?

The system is designed as an academic project supporting driver registration, QR identification, prepaid balance management, terminal transactions, and digital records.

It should not be represented as an official government payment processor, banking service, or official receipt issuer unless the responsible government office formally authorizes those functions.

Does registering in the system automatically authorize a driver to operate?

No. Registration in the system does not automatically establish legal authorization to operate a public utility vehicle. Driver authorization and other regulatory requirements remain subject to the applicable procedures and approval of the responsible office.

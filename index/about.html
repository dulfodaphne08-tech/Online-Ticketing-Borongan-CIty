QR-Based Transport Ticketing
and Fee Collection System

The QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System is a digital platform developed for the Borongan City Transport Terminal to support the organized management of registered public utility vehicle drivers and terminal transactions.

The system brings together driver registration, Driver ID and QR code identification, prepaid balance management, terminal fee processing, digital transaction records, and administrative monitoring within a centralized platform. It is designed to provide authorized personnel with a more organized way to manage driver information and terminal activities while reducing dependence on manual recordkeeping.

01

System Overview

The QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System is a web-based platform that supports the digital management of driver registration, QR-based identification, prepaid balance management, terminal fee processing, and administrative reporting at the Borongan City Transport Terminal.

The system is designed to support the operational and record-management needs of the terminal while keeping the workflow aligned with the policies and approval procedures of the responsible terminal office.

02

Purpose of the System

Description of the Purpose

The system was developed to support the digital management of transportation terminal operations. Its primary purpose is to provide a centralized platform for recording, verifying, and reviewing terminal activity — making records easier to manage, search, verify, and review.

Authorized Personnel Can

  • Maintain registered driver and vehicle information
  • Verify drivers through QR-based identification
  • Record authorized prepaid balance loads
  • Process applicable terminal transportation fees
  • Maintain digital transaction records
  • Monitor terminal activities
  • Generate reports from recorded system data
03

Key Functions

Driver & Vehicle Registration

Records the required information of registered drivers and their associated vehicles.

Driver ID & QR Code

Provides each approved system account with a unique Driver ID and QR code associated with the driver's record.

QR Verification

Allows authorized terminal staff to retrieve registered driver information using the designated QR scanner.

Prepaid Balance

Allows authorized prepaid loads and payment/reference information to be recorded and associated with the driver's account.

Terminal Transactions

Records authorized terminal transactions, applicable fees, deductions, and transaction status.

Reports & Monitoring

Provides authorized administrators with information for reviewing terminal activity and generating reports.

04

Terminal Operations Supported

The system supports the following activities within the digital terminal workflow:

Registration Verification QR Identification Prepaid Balance Terminal Transaction Digital Record Monitoring & Reporting

This workflow allows information recorded during one stage of the process to be used in the succeeding stages. For example, a driver's registered vehicle information can be associated with the driver's QR code. When the QR code is scanned, authorized personnel can retrieve the corresponding record and proceed with the applicable terminal transaction.

05

System Users

The system uses role-based access to separate responsibilities among authorized users.

Driver

Driver

Manages and views their registered account information, Driver ID, QR code, prepaid balance, and transaction history.

Cashier

Cashier

Records authorized prepaid balance loads and associated payment or reference information.

Staff

Staff

Uses the designated scanner to verify registered drivers and process authorized terminal transactions.

Administrator

Administrator

Manages system records, reviews transactions, monitors activities, manages configured fees, and generates reports.

06

Digital Record Management

The system maintains centralized digital records related to driver registration, vehicle information, prepaid balance activity, and terminal transactions. Recorded information may include:

Driver ID
Driver Name
Vehicle Type
Plate Number
Account Status
Date & Time
Applicable Fee
Amount Deducted
Remaining Balance
Payment Ref.
Staff / Cashier
Transaction Status

These records support searching, monitoring, reporting, and authorized administrative review.

07

System Scope

The system focuses on the digital management of driver records, QR-based identification, prepaid balance records, terminal transportation fee transactions, and related reports.

It is intended to support the operational and record-management needs of the transport terminal. The system does not replace the official policies, approval procedures, authorization processes, or responsibilities of the responsible government office.

08

System Boundary

Important System Boundary

The system records authorized payment and reference information and manages prepaid balances and terminal transactions.

It should not be presented as an official government payment processor or official receipt issuer unless the responsible office has formally authorized such functionality.

Actual driver authorization, terminal policies, fee schedules, payment procedures, and official government transactions remain subject to the applicable rules and approval of the responsible office.

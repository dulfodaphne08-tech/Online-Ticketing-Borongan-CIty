System Maintenance and Terminal Fee Schedule Update

The Borongan City Transport Terminal will conduct scheduled system maintenance to support the reliable operation of the QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System. The maintenance may temporarily affect selected system functions.

Published: August 4, 2026 BCTT Operations Office / Terminal System
01

Scheduled Maintenance

The system is scheduled for maintenance on September 15, 2026.

During the maintenance period, selected system functions may be temporarily unavailable while authorized personnel perform system checking, configuration updates, and maintenance activities.

Maintenance Purpose
  • System performance and reliability
  • Database and transaction record checking
  • QR scanning functionality
  • Prepaid balance and transaction processing
  • Fee configuration updates
  • Report generation and monitoring
  • General system security and operational stability
Important

The actual maintenance date and duration are subject to confirmation by the responsible terminal office.

02

System Functions That May Be Affected

Driver Dashboard

Drivers may temporarily be unable to access:

  • Driver information
  • Driver ID
  • QR code
  • Prepaid balance
  • Transaction history
Cashier Functions

Cashiers may temporarily be unable to:

  • Record prepaid balance loads
  • Record payment/reference information
  • Update driver prepaid balances
  • Process pending loading transactions
Staff and QR Scanning

Authorized terminal staff may temporarily be unable to:

  • Scan driver QR codes
  • Retrieve driver information
  • Verify account status
  • Process terminal transactions
  • Record transaction activity
Reports and Monitoring

Authorized administrators may temporarily be unable to:

  • Generate reports
  • Review new transaction records
  • Filter recent transactions
  • Monitor transaction activity
03

What Remains Available

During system maintenance, informational pages may remain accessible depending on the maintenance configuration.

These may include:

  • Homepage
  • About the System
  • Mission & Vision
  • Transport Information
  • Frequently Asked Questions
  • Privacy Policy
  • Security Policy
  • Terms of Use
  • Intellectual Property Rights Policy

System users should wait for the maintenance period to finish before attempting transactions that require database access.

04

Terminal Fee Schedule Update

The system uses a configured fee schedule to determine the applicable terminal fee according to the registered vehicle type.

Important Fee Notice

The amounts shown above are system configuration examples based on the project's current configuration. They should not be treated as an official government fee schedule unless approved and confirmed by the responsible terminal or city office.

05

Before the Maintenance

Drivers, cashiers, and terminal staff should complete necessary activities before the scheduled maintenance period when possible.

Drivers
  • Check your registered driver information.
  • Check your prepaid balance.
  • Complete necessary transactions before maintenance begins.
  • Keep your Driver ID and QR code available.
  • Do not share your username or password with other users.
Cashiers
  • Complete pending prepaid balance loading transactions.
  • Verify payment/reference information.
  • Ensure completed loads are properly recorded.
  • Review pending transactions before maintenance begins.
Terminal Staff
  • Complete pending QR-based transactions when possible.
  • Verify that transaction records are properly saved.
  • Review unresolved transaction issues.
  • Keep necessary transaction references for follow-up.
Administrators
  • Review recent transaction records.
  • Check system activity and pending records.
  • Verify the configured fee schedule.
  • Review reports before the maintenance period if required.
06

After the Maintenance

After the maintenance period, authorized personnel should verify that the system is operating correctly.

System Verification

The following functions should be checked:

1
Driver Login

Confirm that authorized drivers can access their accounts.

2
Driver Information

Verify that registered driver and vehicle information remains correct.

3
QR Verification

Test the designated QR scanner and confirm that registered driver information is retrieved correctly.

4
Prepaid Balance

Verify that driver balances remain accurate after the maintenance.

5
Terminal Transactions

Confirm that authorized transactions can be processed and recorded.

6
Transaction History

Check that completed transactions appear correctly in the driver's and terminal records.

7
Reports

Verify that authorized administrators can view and generate transaction reports.

8
Fee Configuration

Confirm that the configured fee schedule reflects the latest approved information.

07

If You Experience a Problem

If a system function remains unavailable after maintenance, users should report the issue to the responsible terminal personnel.

When reporting a problem, provide information such as:

  • Driver ID, when applicable
  • Date and approximate time of the incident
  • Type of transaction
  • Transaction/reference number, when available
  • Description of the problem
Security Reminder

Never provide your password to another person when reporting a system problem.

Authorized personnel should only request information necessary to identify and resolve the reported issue.

08

Transaction and Balance Reminder

Users should avoid repeating a transaction when the system appears to be processing or temporarily unavailable.

For example, if a prepaid load or terminal transaction appears unsuccessful:

1
Wait for the System Response

Allow the system to complete processing before taking further action.

2
Check Transaction History or Balance

Verify whether the transaction was recorded or the balance was updated.

3
Check for a Reference Number

See whether a transaction/reference number was generated.

4
Contact Authorized Personnel

If the transaction cannot be confirmed, report it to the terminal office.

This helps prevent duplicate transaction records.

09

Important System Boundary

The system is designed to record authorized payment and reference information and to manage prepaid balances and terminal transactions.

It should not be presented as an official government payment processor or official receipt issuer unless the responsible office has formally authorized that function.

The configured fees, maintenance schedules, registration procedures, and operational policies are subject to approval and confirmation by the responsible terminal or city office.

Advisory Effective Sept 1, 2026 Tricycle Drivers

Implementation of QR-Based Fee Collection for Tricycles

The Borongan City Transport Terminal will implement QR-based identification and authorized terminal fee collection for registered tricycle drivers. The process uses the driver's unique QR code to retrieve registered information and record the applicable terminal transaction.

Published: January 23, 2026 Effective: September 1, 2026 Registered Tricycle Drivers
01

Advisory

Starting September 1, 2026, all registered tricycle drivers operating at the Borongan City Transport Terminal will be required to present their assigned QR code during authorized terminal fee collection and verification.

The QR code allows authorized terminal staff to identify the registered driver and retrieve the driver's authorized system information.

02

What This Means for Drivers

Drivers participating in the QR-based terminal process should:

  • Have an active registered driver account.
  • Have an assigned Driver ID and QR code.
  • Present the QR code when requested during an authorized terminal transaction.
  • Maintain sufficient prepaid balance when the transaction requires balance deduction.
  • Ensure that their registered driver and vehicle information is accurate.
  • Follow the procedures provided by authorized terminal personnel.
03

How QR-Based Fee Collection Works

The terminal transaction follows this general process:

1
Present the QR Code

The driver presents the QR code assigned to their registered account.

2
Scan the QR Code

Authorized terminal staff scans the QR code using the designated terminal scanner.

3
Verify Driver Information

The system retrieves the registered driver's information and checks the account status.

4
Check the Applicable Fee

The system identifies the applicable terminal fee based on the configured vehicle type and approved fee schedule.

5
Check Prepaid Balance

When prepaid balance is required, the system checks whether sufficient balance is available.

6
Process the Transaction

If the transaction is authorized and the required balance is available, the applicable amount is deducted from the driver's prepaid balance.

7
Record the Transaction

The system records the transaction in the driver's account and centralized terminal transaction records.

04

Prepaid Balance Requirement

Drivers should maintain sufficient prepaid balance for authorized transactions that require a balance deduction.

Prepaid balance may be loaded through the designated cashier process.

The cashier records the applicable payment or reference information and updates the driver's prepaid balance in the system.

Important

The system records authorized payment and reference information and manages prepaid balances and terminal transactions.

It should not be represented as an official government payment processor or official receipt issuer unless this function has been formally authorized by the responsible office.

05

What Drivers Should Do Before September 1

Drivers should prepare for the implementation by completing the following:

Complete Registration
Ensure that your driver and vehicle information has been submitted and verified.

Secure Your Driver ID and QR Code
Make sure you have received the QR code associated with your registered account.

Check Your Account
Verify that your registered information is correct.

Load Prepaid Balance
If required for terminal transactions, load sufficient prepaid balance through the designated cashier process.

Keep Your QR Code Available
Bring your Driver ID or other authorized QR presentation method when reporting to the terminal.

06

If Your QR Code Cannot Be Scanned

If the QR code cannot be read by the designated scanner:

1
Keep the QR Code Visible

Keep the QR code visible to the authorized staff.

2
Allow Another Scan Attempt

Allow staff to attempt the verification process again.

3
Do Not Create Another Account

Do not create another account without authorization.

4
Contact Terminal Personnel

Contact the responsible terminal personnel if the QR code remains unreadable.

5
Follow Alternate Procedure

Follow any alternate authorized verification procedure provided by the terminal office.

07

If Your Prepaid Balance Is Insufficient

If the system indicates that the available prepaid balance is insufficient:

1
Staff Informs the Driver

The staff will inform the driver of the transaction status.

2
Load Additional Balance

The driver may load additional prepaid balance through the designated cashier process, if applicable.

3
Cashier Records Payment

The cashier records the payment/reference information.

4
Updated Balance Reflected

The updated balance is reflected in the driver's account.

5
Transaction May Proceed

The authorized terminal transaction may then proceed according to the applicable procedure.

Any alternate process must follow the procedure approved by the responsible terminal office.

08

Role of Terminal Staff

Authorized terminal staff are responsible for following the approved QR verification and transaction procedures.

Staff should:

  • Use the designated QR scanner.
  • Verify the correct driver record.
  • Confirm the driver's account status.
  • Check the applicable transaction information.
  • Confirm sufficient balance when required.
  • Record the transaction correctly.
  • Ensure that the transaction is associated with the correct driver.
  • Handle transaction references according to the approved procedure.
09

Transaction Records

Each authorized transaction may be recorded with information such as:

Transaction ID
Driver ID
Driver name
Vehicle type
Plate number
Date and time
Applicable fee
Amount deducted
Remaining balance
Staff responsible
Transaction status
Payment/reference info

These records allow authorized personnel to monitor terminal activity and review transaction history.

10

Important Reminders

Drivers are reminded to:

  • Keep their QR code accessible.
  • Do not share account passwords.
  • Report incorrect driver information to authorized personnel.
  • Report a lost or unreadable QR code promptly.
  • Check their prepaid balance when necessary.
  • Keep transaction or payment references when provided.
  • Follow instructions from authorized terminal personnel.
11

Important System Boundary

The QR-based process is intended for driver identification, verification, authorized terminal transaction processing, prepaid balance management, and digital recordkeeping.

The system does not independently determine whether a driver is legally authorized to operate a public utility vehicle. Driver authorization, registration approval, terminal policies, and applicable fees remain subject to the procedures and approval of the responsible office.

Schedule of Driver Registration for New Applicants

Sample schedule notice for new drivers who need assistance registering for a QR-linked account, including applicants who do not have access to a cellphone or are unable to complete the registration process online.

Published: September 1, 2026 BCTT Operations Office
01

Location:

02

Who Is This Registration For?

This registration assistance is intended for:

  • New drivers who need to create a system account.
  • Drivers who have not yet been registered in the system.
  • Applicants who do not have access to a cellphone.
  • Applicants who have difficulty completing the online registration process.
  • Drivers who require assistance from authorized terminal personnel.

Applicants who can complete the registration process online may follow the designated online registration procedure.

03

What to Bring

Applicants should bring the required documents and information for verification:

  • Valid government-issued identification
  • Valid driver's license
  • Vehicle registration documents, when applicable
  • Vehicle plate number
  • Complete address
  • Contact information
  • Other documents that may be required by the responsible terminal office
Important

Requirements may be changed or supplemented by the responsible office depending on applicable registration procedures.

04

Assisted Registration Process

The following steps describe the assisted registration process for applicants who visit the BCTT Operations Office.

1 Visit the Registration Desk

The applicant visits the BCTT Operations Office during the designated registration schedule.

2 Submit Required Information

The applicant provides the required personal, driver, and vehicle information to the authorized registration personnel.

3 Information Verification

Authorized personnel review the submitted information and documents for completeness and accuracy.

4 Account Registration

Once the information has been properly verified, authorized personnel create or process the applicant's system account.

5 Driver ID & QR Code

After approval, the system generates the driver's unique Driver ID and QR code associated with the registered account.

6 Registration Assistance

Authorized personnel explain how the driver can access and use the QR code during terminal transactions.

05

For Applicants Without a Cellphone

Assisted registration is available for applicants who do not have access to a cellphone or cannot complete the online registration process.

The applicant does not need to complete the online registration process independently. Authorized personnel may assist with entering the required information into the system, subject to the applicable office procedures.

The driver's registration information and QR code remain associated with the driver's approved system record.

06

What Happens After Registration?

After successful registration and approval:

  • The driver's account is recorded in the system.
  • A unique Driver ID is generated.
  • A QR code is associated with the driver's record.
  • The registered vehicle information is linked to the driver.
  • The driver is informed about the proper use of the QR code.
  • The driver may proceed with applicable terminal procedures.

Registration approval remains subject to verification and authorization by the responsible terminal office.

07

Important Reminders

Bring complete and accurate information.
Applicants should ensure that the information provided during registration is correct and matches the submitted documents.

Arrive within the registration hours.
Registration assistance is available only during the designated schedule unless the responsible office announces a different arrangement.

Do not share account credentials.
Drivers should keep their account credentials and QR-related information secure.

Registration does not automatically authorize operation.
Having a registered account or QR code does not by itself constitute government authorization to operate a public utility vehicle. Any required permits, licenses, approvals, and terminal requirements remain subject to the responsible office.

08

Need Assistance?

Applicants who have questions about registration may visit the BCTT Operations Office during registration hours or contact the designated terminal personnel for assistance.

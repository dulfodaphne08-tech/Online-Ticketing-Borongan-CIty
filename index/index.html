ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Borongan City Transport Terminal

The Borongan City Transport Terminal QR-Based Transport Ticketing and Fee Collection System is a digital platform designed to support the management of registered public utility vehicle drivers and terminal transactions.

Driver & Vehicle Registration

Centralized digital records for authorized drivers and their registered vehicles.

QR-Based Identification

Unique QR codes linked to each driver's authorized system record.

Prepaid Balance Management

Authorized prepaid loads and payment reference records.

Terminal Transactions

Authorized staff process and record transactions using the designated QR scanner.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

Mission

The BCTT in collaboration with different government offices and public transport cooperatives/associations shall endeavor to develop and promote the transportation industry as source of livelihood among drivers and operators; ensure the safety, comfort, convenience, and well being of the riding public through the provision of responsive, accountable and God centered regulatory services.

Vision

Consistent with the Vision and Mission of the City Government, the BCTT envisions for a progressive and self-sustaining inter-modal public land transport interchange with God-fearing, highly dedicated and flexible personnel ready to embrace an innovative transportation technology and working towards an inclusive transport business opportunities which shall spur an environment-friendly and sustainable socio-economic growth.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can register?

Authorized public utility vehicle drivers who operate within the applicable terminal procedures may register.

What is the QR code used for?

The QR code is used to identify and verify the registered driver through the terminal's authorized scanning system.

Does the system issue an official government receipt?

The system records authorized payment and reference information. It should only be described as issuing an official government receipt if the responsible office formally authorizes that function.
